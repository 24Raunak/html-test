Why Don't Mountains Grow Forever? Mountains are large natural elevations of the Earth's surface, typically steeper and taller than hills, formed by tectonic forces, volcanism, or erosion. They often occur in long ranges or chains and feature steep slopes and a relatively confined summit or peak. Mountains have a significant impact on local climate and ecosystems, and are used for resource extraction and recreation like climbing and skiing.
